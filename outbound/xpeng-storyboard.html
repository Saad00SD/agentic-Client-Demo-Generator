Before the camera rolls

The first two are specific to this recipient. The rest are the standing rules for any Sherbet prospect video.

The opening line must not sting

“You turned down a call twice, which was fair enough” has to land as genuinely unbothered. If it sounds pointed, the video is dead in five seconds.

Do not name IM Group

Not on screen, not in the narration. Melissa is not named either.

Do not read the numbers

Not one figure is spoken. The pages say the data is illustrative.

Do not say AI

The prepared-answer screen is not in this cut.

Do not open Commercial

The deal is at appointment stage, not proposal. Commercial architecture stays off camera until somebody asks.

Do not add music

The reference is a man turning his laptop round.

Reset between takes

?reset=1 or Shift+0. Clears the drill level and the scroll. English only, so there is no language to clear.

Voice

British, unhurried, slightly flat.

Browser frame

Six tabs, drawn not captured, the demo fifth. Deliberately a different count and order from the Geely video: an identical frame across videos is a template.
