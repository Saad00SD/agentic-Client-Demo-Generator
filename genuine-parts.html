How to participate

A customer who never drove the car is a customer somebody else will get. Order to Delivery puts value behind every order that started with a drive, funded centrally by XPENG UK and administered on the same platform as everything else.

Here is how it works.

  • The UK network converts a drive to an order on of its drives. That is the number to beat.
  • Every order from a customer who drove the car first earns in points.
  • There is no cap on you. The cap is national.
  • The national pot is . When it is dry, the campaign closes, whatever the date.

Points land within five working days of the order being delivered and are spent in the shop.

Attribution rules are listed under T&Cs. The drive must be logged before the order is taken, not added to it afterwards.

Why the pot matters

The pot is what XPENG UK has committed, and everyone on the programme can see it. Campaigns are sized to the pot and can never award more than it holds, so the funding cannot be overspent, and anything unspent carries forward rather than disappearing.

RuleWhat it means

The network conversion rate shown is the average across all participating retailers in the UK over the preceding period. It is published so everyone can see the number they are being measured against, and it is not adjusted mid-period.

A reward of 25 points, worth £25 to a UK participant, is issued for each qualifying order with a completed drive attached, in the order in which invoices are confirmed across the UK, until the pot of 120,000 points is exhausted. The pot is held in points because a point is worth one unit of local currency in whichever market it is spent.

This campaign is open to orders taken by an enrolled individual at a participating XPENG retail retailer. Staff and family drives, demonstrator movements and drives logged without a named prospect are excluded from both the conversion rate and the reward calculation.

XPENG UK reserves the right to withhold or reclaim any reward where there are reasonable grounds to suspect manipulation, including but not limited to parts added to an invoice and credited afterwards, or an order backdated onto a drive that never happened. Rewards reclaimed are deducted from the individual's points balance.

Two sources confirm a qualifying order: the sales manager attributes it to an individual, and the sales system confirms the parts line on the same invoice. Nothing is paid until both agree.
