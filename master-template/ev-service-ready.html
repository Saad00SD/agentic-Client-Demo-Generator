How to participate

Product Certification runs continuously and covers four modules. Each stands on its own, and you can take them in any order.

  • Every module completed earns in points.
  • Finishing all four releases a £140 completion award on top of the module points.
  • Modules take about forty minutes each and can be done between appointments.
  • Certification also feeds your retailer's score in The Retailer League.

You do not need to register. Every enrolled sales executive is entered automatically.

Completion is written back to the distributor's training record the moment you finish a module. Nothing is self-declared.

Terms & Conditions

Open continuously from the date a retailer is appointed. Modules do not close at the end of a quarter.

Each module ends in a short assessment. A module counts as complete when the assessment is passed, and it does not expire.

ModuleWhat it coversWorth

Certification carries forward. Where a module is materially updated, for example on a new battery chemistry or a change to isolation procedure, the affected module reopens and is payable again.

A reward of 35 points, worth £35 to a UK participant, is issued on completion of each module, credited within one working day. The 140-point completion award is issued once all four are complete.

XPENG UK reserves the right to withhold or reclaim rewards where an assessment appears to have been completed by someone other than the enrolled individual.

Completions are taken from the distributor's training record and are the single accepted source for this campaign.
