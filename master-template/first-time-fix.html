How it works

Every completed drive counts, confirmed against the prospect record in the sales system. A drive that cannot be matched to a named prospect is removed in the week it is found, which is why the reward is credited a month in arrears rather than on the day.

The rate is not marginal. Clear Accelerate on your sixteenth drive and all sixteen earn at the higher rate, which is why the last week of the month is worth more than the first.

What the points are

One point is one pound. Spend them in the shop on gift cards, travel, experiences or the signature tier. Your whole balance is open to every category, with nothing ring-fenced.

No cash changes hands. Points are earned by you and spent by you. Nothing is paid through your employer's payroll and no cash-out route exists, which is what keeps the programme simple to run.

Terms & Conditions

Test Drive runs continuously. Each period begins at 00:01 on the first calendar day of the month and closes at 23:59 on the last. The September 2026 period closes on 30 September 2026.

Qualifying drives are test drives completed by an enrolled individual at a participating XPENG retailer and completed. A return for the same drive found to have been logged without a customer is netted off in the week it is found.

The following are excluded and do not contribute to the ladder: staff and family drives, demonstrator movements, and drives logged without a named prospect.

Only drives attributed to the enrolled individual will count. Drives recorded under a different account, including a second account held at the same site, are not aggregated.

Band targets are calculated from the account's own rolling twelve-month drive average and are loaded automatically at the start of each period. Targets are not comparable between accounts, and no account competes against another.

BandTargetReward

At the close of each period the highest band reached is unlocked and the corresponding reward is credited to the individual's points balance within five working days of results being published.

Points are redeemed in the reward shop. There is no cash-out route and no payment is made through the employer's payroll.

XPENG UK reserves the right to withhold or reclaim rewards where there are reasonable grounds to suspect that qualifying drives have been manipulated, including drives logged across multiple lines solely to reach a band.

Two sources confirm a qualifying drive: the sales manager attributes it to an individual, and the sales system confirms the prospect record at invoice. Where a discrepancy is raised, the sales system is final.
