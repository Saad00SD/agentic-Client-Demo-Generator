`FACT` means a source states it directly. `INFERENCE` means it is my reasoning from the facts
above it. Nothing here is invented.

one sender. The video's whole power is that it looks singular and considered. Four threads is
the one thing that reliably destroys that.

### What that pattern actually means

`INFERENCE`. Two declines and an ignored follow-up is not disinterest in the category. If he
were uninterested he would have said so once. Declining twice and then going quiet is what a
busy person does when the *meeting* is the problem, not the subject: a call about a platform
he has not seen costs him thirty minutes to find out whether it is worth thirty minutes.

That is precisely the problem a fifty-second video removes, and it is the same shape as the
Harry Bathe situation on the Geely side.

**`INFERENCE`, and the basis of the video:** XPENG does not employ most of the people who sell
its cars. No source states a network-wide percentage, so this is a pattern across every
disclosed market rather than a quotable statistic, and it should be phrased that way. It makes
the measurement question sharper than it is for a manufacturer with its own subsidiaries:
you cannot manage what you do not employ, so you had better be able to see it.

## 6. Verdicts

### WHY THIS ACCOUNT

An open deal at appointment stage with a warm advocate inside it, stalled for ten weeks on one
person who will not take a call. That is a better-qualified opportunity than anything a cold
approach produces, and it is closer to revenue than the three other XPENG threads combined.

### WHY NOW

Nothing has moved since 18 June. The next step logged in HubSpot is "re-chase Jonny Miller for
a workable time," and re-chasing with the same ask that has already been declined twice is the
one approach with evidence against it.

### WHY SHERBET

Sherbet runs the BYD UK programme, live since March 2026 on a multi-year agreement, and the
Alexander Wass case study Melissa has already seen. The credential is established with her.
It does not need re-establishing with him inside a fifty-second video.

### REASON NOT TO SEND

Melissa may have moved on, or may have had an internal answer she did not pass on. If the
video reaches her and nothing happens, that is information: it says the block is not Jonny's
diary, and the account needs a different route rather than a better asset.

The other reason to hold: if anyone at Sherbet has approached XPENG again since 21 August and
not logged it, this becomes the fifth thread rather than a revival of the first. Worth thirty
seconds of checking before it goes.
