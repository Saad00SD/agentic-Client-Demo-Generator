How to participate

Every participating retailer in the network is entered automatically at the start of the quarter. There is nothing to register for.

Four measures, ten points each.

  • Test drive rate. Share of enquiries that reach a completed drive, with the customer at the wheel.
  • Order conversion. Share of completed drives that convert to a signed order.
  • EV readiness. Sales executives fully certified as a share of the site.
  • Customer satisfaction. Handover survey in the first ninety days, averaged across the quarter.

Each measure is scored against your retailer's own baseline, so a two-person retailer is not competing against a twenty-person one on raw throughput. The league rewards improvement, not size.

Standings are marked provisional until the quarter closes and the final sales data is reconciled.

Terms & Conditions

1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026. Standings publish weekly and are marked provisional until the quarter is reconciled.

The campaign carries two leagues. The retailer league ranks retailers on four operational measures. The individual league ranks enrolled participants on the points they have earned in the period, within their own market.

Individuals are ranked by name within the UK network only. No individual is ranked across regions, because a market running more campaigns would win on volume rather than on performance. Nothing about an individual's pay, employment or appraisal is part of either league.

All participating retailers in the network are entered automatically. Retailers appointed part way through a quarter are entered from the following quarter, so that a full baseline exists.

Each of the four measures is scored from one to ten against the retailer's own baseline, and the four are summed to a total out of forty. Ties are separated by test drive rate.
