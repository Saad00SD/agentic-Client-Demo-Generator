How to participate

Demonstrator Days runs every month and everyone at the desk is entered automatically. There is nothing to register for and nothing to claim.

Here is how it works.

  • Get the demonstrator out with a customer. That is the whole behaviour.
  • Clear across at least in the month and the award is paid in full.
  • It is not a sliding scale. Either the month qualifies or it does not.
  • The UK network has its demonstrator out on of its drives.

Points land within five working days of the month closing and are spent in the shop.

A booking cancelled on the day is removed from the count in the week it is cancelled, which is why the award is paid a month in arrears rather than on the day.

Why a threshold and not a ladder

A ladder rewards volume, and volume is the wrong answer here: an executive with sixty enquiries should not beat one with twenty simply by seeing more people. A rate with a minimum day count measures the behaviour rather than the size of the diary, and the minimum stops a quiet month producing a flattering percentage on four days.

Campaigns on this programme deliberately use different mechanisms. A network that only ever sees ladders learns to chase whatever the ladder counts.

Terms & Conditions

Demonstrator Days runs continuously. Each period begins at 00:01 on the first calendar day of the month and closes at 23:59 on the last. The September 2026 period closes on 30 September 2026.

A day qualifies when the demonstrator is booked out to a named customer and confirmed in the management system before the vehicle is collected. A booking made after collection, by telephone or by any other route, does not count towards this campaign.

RuleWhat it means

The award is paid where the availability rate is at or above the published threshold and the individual has recorded at least the minimum number of qualifying working days in the period. Both conditions must be met.

The threshold is published at the start of the period and is not adjusted mid-period. The Network average is shown for context and does not affect the calculation.

The award is a fixed monthly amount, credited to the individual's points balance within five working days of the period closing. It is not pro-rated and does not increase with a higher rate.

Points are redeemed in the reward shop. There is no cash-out route and no payment is made through the employer's payroll.

XPENG UK reserves the right to withhold or reclaim rewards where booked days appear to have been created to reach the threshold, including booked days cancelled shortly after the period closes.

Both the working day count and the booked day count are taken from the sales system, which is the single accepted source for this campaign.

These terms are illustrative demonstration copy written to show the structure of a live campaign's rules. They are not a legal document and have not been reviewed.
